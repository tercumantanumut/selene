Backups and decision rules

Heavy rain or flooded roads after the sanctuary

Skip Old Town completely. Leave Paklok around 13:15 and return directly to Patong. The sanctuary is the main experience; Old Town is optional.

Direct rain route
Morning program is sold out or cannot be confirmed

Do not substitute an elephant-riding, bathing or feeding venue merely to preserve the theme. First ask Phuket Elephant Sanctuary about:

  1. The official 13:30 half-day program, if still available.
  2. The 1.5-hour Canopy Walkway Tour, officially priced from ฿1,900.
  3. A wait-list or cancellation space.

If none is available, switch to a rain-resilient Old Phuket Town food-and-architecture day rather than a hands-on elephant camp.

Low-energy version

Request golf-cart assistance at the sanctuary, then return directly to Patong after the included lunch. Total outing becomes approximately 07:30–14:30 with no Old Town walking.

Old Town shops close for the public holiday

Keep the walk exterior-only: Soi Romanee, Thalang Road arcades and Phang Nga Road. Use any visibly open hotel or café for a drink and restroom, then return early. Do not rely on Wednesday museum access.

Animal welfare, cultural etiquette and safety

  • Follow the guide and maintain the requested distance from elephants.
  • Do not ask for touching, riding, feeding, bathing or posed interactions.
  • Do not use flash or block an elephant’s route.
  • At shrines, cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes where requested, keep voices low and avoid photographing worshippers without permission.
  • Wet pavements and painted road markings become slippery. Use pedestrian crossings carefully and assume drivers may not yield.
  • Do not walk through moving or deep water. Ask the driver to turn back if water is pooling across the road.
  • Tourist Police: 1155. Emergency medical service: 1669.
