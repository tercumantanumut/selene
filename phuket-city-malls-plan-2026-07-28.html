Solo-friendly practical rules

  • Use app-booked cars, confirm the plate and share ride status. Decline unsolicited taxi changes after entering the car.
  • Wait for pickup inside the mall until the vehicle is close; use the mall’s designated pickup area.
  • Keep handbag zipped and worn in front in crowded areas. Do not leave the phone on a café table.
  • For expensive purchases, verify the final card amount in baht before tapping and retain receipts.
  • Do not walk through moving or ankle-deep road water; remain inside and request a different pickup point.
  • Tourist Police: 1155. Medical emergency: 1669. Central Floresta: +66 76 603 333.
